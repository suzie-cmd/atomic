Does it hurt?

It stings, but it's fast. Most sessions take 10–30 minutes, and clients regularly describe it as “quick and minimal pain” or “almost pain free.” We'll walk you through comfort options at your consult so you know exactly what to expect.

Will it scar?

The laser is designed to break up ink, not damage the skin around it. How well you heal depends mostly on aftercare: keep it clean, stay out of the sun, and don't pick at scabs. We give you detailed aftercare instructions after every session.

Is it safe for my skin tone?

Yes. Darker skin tones may need gentler laser settings to avoid pigment changes, which can mean a few more sessions. We tailor every plan to your skin type and walk you through it at your consult.

Can you remove colored ink?

The Astanza Trinity uses three wavelengths, so it can target black, red, orange, and the stubborn blues and greens that single-wavelength lasers struggle with.

Can I just fade it for a cover-up?

Absolutely. Fading usually takes fewer sessions than full removal. Tell us your artist's plan and we'll build around it.

Cellulite treatments

The non-invasive solution to cellulite and dimpling.

If you're looking to reduce the appearance of cellulite and achieve smoother, firmer skin, we offer advanced, non-invasive treatments designed to target and minimize it.

Cellulite dimpling occurs when fibrotic septa, or bands of connective tissue, pull down on the skin. It's often caused by genetics, hormonal changes, and lifestyle. Our advanced energy-based treatments break down these bands and stimulate collagen production to smooth and tighten the skin.

Before you book

Good questions. Straight answers.

Does it hurt?

It stings, but it's quick. Most sessions take 10–30 minutes, and clients often describe it as “quick and minimal pain.” We'll walk you through comfort options at your consult.

How many sessions will I need?

Most tattoos take 6–12 sessions for full removal. Highly saturated, layered, or cover-up tattoos may need more. You'll get an honest estimate at your free consultation.

How far apart are sessions?

6–8 weeks. Your skin needs to heal fully and your body needs time to flush the shattered ink. Rushing doesn't speed things up.

What will the whole thing cost?

Multiply your per-session price by 6–12 sessions. For example, a small tattoo ($150/session) usually lands between $900 and $1,800, before package savings of up to 15%. See the pricing page for every size.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. Flexible monthly payments through Cherry are available on packages, and we accept all major credit cards.

Will it scar?

The laser targets ink, not the skin around it. Healing well comes down to aftercare: keep it clean, avoid sun, moisturize, and don't pick at scabs. You'll get detailed instructions after every session.

Can you remove colored ink?

Yes. The Trinity's three wavelengths target black (1064 nm), reds, oranges, and browns (532 nm), and stubborn greens, blues, and purples (694 nm).

Is it safe for darker skin?

Yes. Darker skin tones may need lower laser settings to avoid pigment changes, which can add sessions. Your plan is tailored to your skin type.

Can I fade it for a cover-up instead?

Definitely. Fading for a cover-up usually takes fewer sessions than full removal.

Do you remove permanent makeup?

Yes, including eyebrows and lip liner. See permanent makeup removal, or text us a photo and we'll tell you what's possible.

What happens at the consultation?

It's free and there's no obligation. We review your tattoo, skin type, and goals, then give you an estimated treatment plan and your exact price.
